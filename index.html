Method

Desk rules: a line appears only with a source link, an as-of date and a revision note. When a value changes, the old line stays visible in the history, struck through. Values are copied from the source, never typed from memory. Price lines carry the source's own as-of date and publication lag.

Disclaimers

Not investment advice. A record of public information, not a recommendation to buy, sell or build.

No real-time coverage. The rail refreshes about every 6 hours; desks change only when a sourced change lands; public series publish with a lag.

PROTOTYPE marks example data for UX testing: fiction, not sourced, not part of any desk or subscription.
